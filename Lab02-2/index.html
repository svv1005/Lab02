About Us

Founded in 2021, Brew & Bloom Café is a locally owned coffee house dedicated to ethically sourced beans, seasonal ingredients, and a warm atmosphere. Our baristas treat every cup like a small work of art, and our garden patio is the perfect spot to slow down.

  • Locally roasted, fair-trade coffee
  • Fresh pastries baked every morning
  • Free Wi-Fi and cozy seating
  • Pet-friendly outdoor patio
